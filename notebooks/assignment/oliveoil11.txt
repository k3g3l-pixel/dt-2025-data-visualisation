Mature olive oil: 
Olive oil obtained from olives harvested when ripe. 
Generally speaking, it is mildly fruity,
 with sweet notes and only a hint of bitterness and spiciness.
 It is the mildest, sweetest and least spicy olive oil.
 It pairs well with mildly flavoured soups,
  boiled vegetables with subtle flavours,
   eggs, mushrooms, dainty desserts,
    light cold salads, tender fish,
     mild sauces, boiled potatoes and chestnuts.
